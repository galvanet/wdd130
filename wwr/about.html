Whitewater Rafting-GDL

Whitewater is one of GDL's greatest assets. Rivers and rapids of all sizes are a delight for rafting enthusiasts. Climb aboard an unsinkable inflatable raft piloted by whitewater savvy guides and get ready to experience a whole range of emotions as well as a dip or two in the river! With family or friends, whether you're a beginner or an expert, you can count on our network of Quality-Safety certified businesses for a memorable rafting experience in GDL.

History

More than 60 years of rafting on GDL's rivers is enough time to experience anything that can happen on a whitewater rafting trip.

Three generations of GDL's and the staff welcome more than 10,000 guests on river trips throughout GDL each year!
